Our story

Na Cait Shràide's (The Alley Cats') name and imagery come half from history and half from a fictional Gaelic tale: this one is about the sabermxn, which is often mistaken for a werewolf, but it's far more dangerous, hunting every night.

The tale is set in late 15th-century Scotland. After the death of John Calder, Thane of Cawdor Castle, the powerful Campbells seized the chance to absorb the Calder lineage by kidnapping John's daughter Muriel, the rightful heiress. But some details don't add up: few sources mention Muriel's missing older sister...

Come follow Na Cait Shràide on the chase for Janet Calder, the true heir of the Thane of Cawdor! It is a journey that collapses Scottish timelines to the beat of industrial goth, where a true tale woven with myth unfolds. Katana and Alejandro make the tunes in Strudel, with a custom tartan-reactive visualizer built in L5.
